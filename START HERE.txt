NIGHTFIRE PC ALPHA - START HERE
===============================

This is the source of the 007 Nightfire Windows port. It contains no game: Setup builds the
two engines on your PC from your own PAL Xbox disc.

1. Install, if you do not have them yet:
   - Python 3.8 or newer (https://www.python.org/downloads/; tick "Add python.exe to PATH").
   - Git for Windows (https://git-scm.com/download/win).
   - Build Tools for Visual Studio 2022 (https://visualstudio.microsoft.com/downloads/, under
     "Tools for Visual Studio") with the "Desktop development with C++" workload.
2. Copy the contents of your extracted PAL disc into release\alpha\Game Files, so that
   default.xbe and Driving.xbe sit directly inside it.
3. Double-click release\alpha\Setup.cmd. The first run downloads the translator, translates
   the game's code and compiles both engines: about 15 minutes on a recent laptop, longer on
   slower PCs, and about 2 GB of space.
4. Play with release\alpha\Play Nightfire.cmd.

release\alpha\README.txt explains the other launchers, the controls, the PC settings (F10),
known issues and what to send if something goes wrong. Everything outside release\alpha is the
port's source code.

The engines Setup builds are made from the game's own code: once Setup has run, do not share or
upload this folder.
